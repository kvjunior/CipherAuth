# STOC 2027 submission readiness

Date: 2026-10-29

## Format decision

This package is a **full-submission draft**, not an accepted camera-ready
proceedings version. Under the STOC 2027 call, a full submission has no total
page limit, but its main merits must appear within the first 12 pages after the
title page. The accepted proceedings version is limited to 12 pages in ACM
`sigconf` format.

The draft uses US letter paper, 11-point type, one column, single spacing, and
one-inch margins. The title page and table of contents precede Arabic page 1.
The model, literature positioning, complete construction, two game definitions,
unconditional attack, positive theorem statement, reduction roadmap, scope
composition, artifact evidence, limitations, and conclusion appear by Arabic
page 10. Appendices and references follow.
